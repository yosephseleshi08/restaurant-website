TIPS FOR SENDING DMs
================================================================================

1. PERSONALIZE EVERY MESSAGE
   - Use their actual name
   - Mention their actual restaurant name
   - Reference something specific (a dish, a recent post, their location)

2. SEND AT THE RIGHT TIME
   - Restaurant owners: 10-11 AM or 2-4 PM (between rushes)
   - Agencies: 9-11 AM on weekdays
   - Avoid Friday afternoons and weekends

3. FOLLOW UP ONCE
   - Day 1: Initial DM
   - Day 4: Follow-up (Script 3)
   - Day 10: Final check-in with demo link
   - Then move on. Do not spam.

4. HAVE A DEMO READY
   - Deploy your platform on Render/Railway (free tier)
   - Load it with Italian demo data
   - Send the link immediately when they say "yes"

5. TRACK YOUR NUMBERS
   - 50 DMs sent
   - 10 replies (20%)
   - 5 demos (10%)
   - 1-2 sales (2-4%)
   - Target: 1 sale per 25-50 DMs

6. DO NOT SELL IN THE FIRST MESSAGE
   - Lead with value (save money, keep revenue)
   - Ask a question (worth a look?)
   - Offer a demo (low commitment)
